Prototypes for the "where most health care savings come from" module, built on the ROI study (13,548 members, $2.39 per $1). Use the picker at the top-left of any page to switch colorway (Navy, Navy Solid, Neutral, Sky).

  • Scroll

    The module pins while you scroll through a year of care: 100 members step down a level, savings build from $0.00 to $2.39, and the iceberg closer shows the return the study didn't measure.

  • Spotlight

    The same 100 members under two approaches. Adaptive care lights everyone; therapy-first lights only the 12 high-severity members and leaves the moderate 60% of savings empty.

    NavyNeutral

  • Staircase

    Static, no interaction. Costs rise as steps from low to high severity, with members as dots inside each step and the savings from each step down between them.
