Possible additional return · not measured in our ROI study

  • Productivity at work 1

    Workplace costs, like missed work and reduced performance, make up 61% of the U.S. economic burden of depression.

  • Fewer lost workdays 2

    An estimated 12 billion workdays are lost to depression and anxiety worldwide each year.

  • Symptoms that don’t escalate 3

    In one study, most adults with untreated mild depression had major depression four years later.

Items below the waterline are possible additional return, not measured in Modern Health’s ROI study; figures there come from outside research, not Modern Health results. 1. Greenberg et al., PharmacoEconomics, 2021. 2. WHO and ILO, 2022. 3. Weissman et al., Psychiatric Services, 2010.
